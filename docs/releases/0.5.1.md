Codex Meter 0.5.1 makes reset posts easier to read and improves CLI discovery.

- Tibo posts now include a short excerpt around **reset**, highlighted in bold. The excerpt preserves whole words, uses up to 72 characters and wraps to at most two lines.
- CLI discovery falls back to the Codex desktop app when standalone paths are unavailable, including broken symlinks after desktop updates. Valid standalone installations keep priority.
- Updated README preview with synthetic quota values and a fictional post, plus clearer update instructions.

## Install

Download **Codex-Meter-0.5.1-macos-arm64.zip**, extract it, quit the old copy, and replace the app in Applications. Requires **Apple Silicon, macOS 13+**, and a signed-in Codex CLI (standalone or bundled with Codex desktop).

Version 0.5.0 can show a blue update dot after its next update check. Choose **Check for Updates…** for an immediate check. Download and installation remain manual.

The app is **ad hoc signed and not notarized by Apple**. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/emmepra/codex-meter/blob/v0.5.1/docs/INSTALL.md) for Apple's per-app approval procedure. Verify the archive with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Reset posts still use the third-party Nitter feed and may be delayed or unavailable. A keyword match does not confirm an account reset. Native login behavior has not been tested through a full logout/login cycle.
